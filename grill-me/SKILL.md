# Grill me

Interview the user relentlessly about every aspect of their plan until you reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one.

Ask one question at a time. Never bundle multiple questions into a single message.

For each question, provide your recommended answer alongside the question. The user should be reacting to a concrete proposal, not starting from scratch.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase rather than asking the user, do that instead — don't waste questions on things you can verify yourself.

Continue until every meaningful decision in the plan has been surfaced and resolved.
